- Before writing code for anything non-trivial, describe your plan and the key decision first.
- After a change, summarise the 1 or 2 most important decisions and why, not every line.
- I am learning. When I ask to be quizzed, ask one question at a time and give no hints or answers until I've tried.
- Plain JavaScript only. Don't add TypeScript, and don't add new dependencies without flagging them.
- Keep changes small enough that the main decision fits in one sentence.
- I'm learning to create Claude Code skills. When a task or workflow could be turned into a skill (something repeatable, multi-step, or that I keep explaining), flag it and briefly say why it would make a good skill.
